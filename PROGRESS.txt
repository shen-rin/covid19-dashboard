[STEP 2 — DATA STORY]  ✓ Defined
  Main message:
    Russia officially recorded 403 000 COVID deaths — highest in Europe —
    while vaccinating only 61% of its population (~20 pp below EU average).
  Supporting message 1:
    The Omicron wave (Jan 2022) drove global weekly cases to 6.3M+,
    yet death rates fell sharply everywhere as vaccines reduced severity.
  Supporting message 2:
    Russia's vaccination rate (61%) trailed France (81%) and Germany (78%)
    by 20 percentage points, correlating directly with higher mortality.
