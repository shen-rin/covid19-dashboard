Main Finding

Russia officially recorded 403 000 COVID deaths — the highest toll in Europe — while vaccinating only 61% of its population, nearly 20 points below the EU average. Independent demographic estimates put Russia's true excess mortality at 2–3× the official figure.

The Omicron wave drove global new cases to a weekly record of 6.3 million. Yet death rates fell sharply worldwide as vaccines and prior immunity blunted severity — except in under-vaccinated populations.
−20 pp
Russia's vaccination rate peaked at 61% vs 81% in France and 78% in Germany — a 20-point gap. Lower uptake of Sputnik V, combined with vaccine hesitancy, left millions unprotected during the Delta and Omicron waves.
