COVID-19 Global Analysis · 2020 – 2024

Russia Led Europe in Deaths,
Not in Vaccines

WHO / Our World in Data · 40 countries · 4+ years · ~430 000 data points

Main Finding

Russia officially recorded 403 000 COVID deaths — the highest toll in Europe — while vaccinating only 61% of its population, nearly 20 points below the EU average.

Independent demographic estimates put Russia's true excess mortality at 2–3× the official figure.

Russia vs EU
Russia's vaccination coverage peaked at 61% — roughly 20 percentage points below France (81%) and Germany (78%). Slow rollout of Sputnik V and widespread vaccine hesitancy left a large share of the population exposed during the deadliest waves, contributing to Europe's highest official COVID death toll.

Omicron Peak
In January 2022 the Omicron variant pushed weekly global cases to an all-time peak of 6.3 million. Despite the scale of the wave, deaths rose far more slowly — vaccines and prior infection had already blunted the virus's lethality in most of the world.
Dec 2022 Wave
In December 2022 China abruptly ended its Zero-COVID policy, triggering an estimated 1 billion infections within weeks — the largest single-country outbreak of the pandemic. Simultaneously, the XBB subvariant drove renewed waves across Europe and North America, though global mortality remained far below earlier peaks thanks to widespread immunity and updated vaccines.
